## 2. Data processed by the App

The App requires **no sign-up and no account**. The Developer does not collect or receive your name,
phone number, e-mail, address, location, contacts or files.

Game data is stored **locally on your device**: best score, crystals, unlocked and selected comets,
last daily gift date and streak, sound/music/vibration settings, first-run tutorial flag and service
values for the ad slot (banner height, impression pacing). This data never leaves the device,
is not sent to the Developer and is removed together with the App.

## 3. Ads and third-party SDK

The App shows ads via the **Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK)**, which acts as an
independent controller for the data it processes to serve and measure ads: advertising identifier
(GAID/OAID), IP address and coarse region derived from it, device model, OS version, system language,
network type, screen size, impressions and clicks.

Purposes: ad delivery (including personalisation unless disabled), frequency capping, fraud prevention,
ad measurement.

You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings: "Settings → Google → Ads".

Partner document: Yandex privacy policy — https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/

## 4. Permissions

`INTERNET` (loading ads) and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (checking connectivity before an ad request).
The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, files, location or SMS.

## 5. Sharing

The Developer does not sell or share user data. The only third-party interaction is the ad SDK
described in section 3.

## 6. Retention

Local game data is kept until you delete the App or clear its data. Ad SDK data is retained
according to Yandex's policy.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 6 and does not knowingly collect children's data.
Store age rating: 6+.

## 8. Your rights

You may request information about processing, rectification, blocking or deletion of data, and withdraw
consent. Since the Developer stores no personal data, the main way to exercise these rights is to delete
the App or clear its data; ad-related requests can be sent to the contact e-mail and will be forwarded
to the partner.

## 9. Security

Game data lives in the App's sandboxed SharedPreferences, inaccessible to other apps without root.
Ad requests are sent over HTTPS.
