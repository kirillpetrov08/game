Orbita is a neon arcade game about a comet racing along the orbits around a star. One tap switches the orbit, one wrong move ends the run. The controls take five seconds to learn and the skill ceiling is high — there is always a better score to chase.

🎮 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the left half of the screen to move one orbit closer to the centre.
• Tap the right half to move one orbit outwards.
• Four orbits, smooth transitions, a comet trail and particles.

✨ WHAT'S INSIDE
• Crystals for score, a combo multiplier up to x5 and a "graze" bonus for passing dangerously close to hazards.
• Red barriers, shrinking rings and meteor showers. Every threat is telegraphed: barriers pulse before they switch on, meteors are marked on the screen edge.
• Fair difficulty: a barrier never activates while the player is inside its zone.
• Power-ups: shield (absorbs one hit), magnet (pulls crystals in) and time slow.
• A new level every 15 seconds: the pace and the colour of the scene keep changing.
• Eight comets, from the classic Nova to the rainbow Prism. Unlock them with crystals or with a streak of daily gifts.

🎁 NO STRINGS ATTACHED
• Free to play, no sign-up, no in-app purchases.
• A daily gift for showing up — crystals for new comets.
• Ads: a banner at the bottom, optional rewarded video (continue a run, bonus crystals) and an ad on launch. Everything is skippable, rewarded video is always opt-in.
• You can continue one run after losing by watching a video.

⚙️ SETTINGS AND LANGUAGE
• Separate toggles for sound, music and vibration.
• Russian and English UI, following the system language.
• A "How to play" screen on the first launch.

📱 TECH
• Plays offline: the internet is only used to load ads.
• About 5 MB, no heavy assets — all audio and graphics are generated on the device.
• Android 7.0 and newer, tuned for portrait phone screens.
• No personal data collection: scores and crystals stay on the device.

Orbita is a perfect two-minute run: in a queue, on the subway or before bed. Try to beat your own record — and don't forget the graze bonus.
